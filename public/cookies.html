Cookie & Telemetry Disclosure

Last Updated: October 2026 | Technical Disclosure

1. How We Use Cookies

The SAPVIK website is built on high-performance static architecture and operates with minimal cookie dependencies. We use essential session cookies and browser storage (such as sessionStorage) exclusively to maintain your audit tool results, preserve form progress, and track anonymous referral attribution (e.g. UTM campaign tags).

2. Analytics Infrastructure

We utilize aggregated performance metrics to evaluate page load speeds, user navigation friction, and scanner tool usage. We never link analytics sessions to identifiable personal records without your explicit form submission.

3. Managing Preferences

You can adjust your browser settings at any time to refuse cookies or remove existing stored records. Disabling cookies will not impair your ability to browse our services or submit a growth consultation request.
